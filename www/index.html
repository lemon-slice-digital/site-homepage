Privacy

We take privacy very seriously, that's why we

  • don't collect any personal information at all,
  • don't use cookies on this website,
  • don't use external services analytics or tracking tools,
  • never store your ip address anywhere.

Building on these principles, we

  • aggregate browser information like origin countries, device types and screen-sizes,
  • only reference fully anonymized hashes as same-sessions identifiers,
  • host the site and tools locally, without hosting services or sub-processors

If you are concerned about fundamental browsing interaction information being exposed, we recommend investing a virtual private network like Mozilla VPN that protects your connection to the internet, keeping your location and what you do online more private across your devices.
